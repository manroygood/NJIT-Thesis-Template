\newcommand{\stylename}{ASCE}
\newcommand{\stylecode}{numeric}
\newcommand{\stylefeatures}{%
  \item \texttt{biblatex} has no ASCE style, so this page uses the generic \texttt{numeric} style. ASCE journals cite by author and year, so check your references against the ASCE guide in Appendix~B of the sample.
  \item Citations are numbers in square brackets, [1]; the reference list is in alphabetical order and numbered in that order.
  \item Initials come before the family name; article titles are in quotation marks; book and journal titles are italic.
  \item An article ends with \emph{In: Journal 11.11 (1981), pp.~1119--1184}.}
