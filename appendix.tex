\oneappendix{Cell Signaling and Computational Biology Resources}
\label{app:single}

NJIT enforces a strict rule regarding the titling and numbering of appendices:
\begin{itemize}
\item If your dissertation has only a \textbf{single appendix}, it must be titled \textbf{APPENDIX} (without any letter label such as "A"), followed by the appendix title.
\item If your dissertation contains \textbf{two or more appendices}, they must be numbered sequentially as \textbf{APPENDIX A}, \textbf{APPENDIX B}, etc.
\end{itemize}

Removing the letter label manually from a standard \LaTeX\ chapter command often breaks numbering for equations, figures, and tables inside the appendix. To solve this, \texttt{dmsthesis.cls} provides the dedicated \verb+\oneappendix{Title}+ command. It sets the centered title correctly, adds the entry to the Table of Contents as \texttt{APPENDIX: TITLE}, and properly configures subordinate numbering.

University guidelines also require that every appendix begin with introductory text before any figures or tables appear.
