\chapter{Citation style guides and examples}
\label{app:citestyles}

This appendix supports Section~\ref{sec:citestyles}. Table~\ref{tab:style-resources} points to the official guide, or a reliable summary, for each citation style the GSO accepts, and Table~\ref{tab:citation-styles} shows how one journal article is cited in each style. Use them to check the output of the style you set with \verb+\mycitestyle+ in \texttt{packages.tex}.
